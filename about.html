About Northern Trails

A small team of mountain people who love sharing their home.

Our story

Started around a kitchen table

Northern Trails began when a group of friends from Gilgit, Skardu and Lahore got tired of watching visitors rush past the places they loved. We wanted trips where there is time for a second cup of tea, a village walk and a conversation with the people who live there.

Today we are a small agency that still plans every trip by hand and still hires guides from the valleys we visit.

What guides us

Our values

🌱

Responsible travel

We keep groups small, avoid single-use plastics and ask guests to leave places as they found them.

🤝

Community first

We use family-run guesthouses and local suppliers so tourism income stays in the valley.

💡

Honesty

Clear prices, clear expectations and an open conversation when weather changes the plan.
