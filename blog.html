Trekking

Hiking to Fairy Meadows: what to expect

  • By Imran, lead guide
  • 5 min read

The hike from the roadhead takes most people three to five hours, climbing steadily through pine forest. Start early, carry more water than you think you need and rest often. Horses and porters are available for bags. The reward is a meadow with a clear view of Nanga Parbat when the clouds lift, so allow at least one night to wait for the best light.

Seasons

Why October is a secret favourite in Hunza

  • By Nadia, trip designer
  • 4 min read

Crowds thin out, the air turns crisp and the poplars and apricot trees glow gold and red. Days are shorter and nights are cold, so pack warm layers, but the mountains are often at their clearest. If you enjoy photography and quiet lanes, autumn is hard to beat.

Tips

Five packing mistakes first-time visitors make

  • By Tariq, operations
  • 3 min read

Bringing only summer clothes, new boots that have not been worn in, too many bags for small vehicles, no cash for remote villages and no offline maps. A small day pack, layers you can add and remove, and a little patience for slow roads will serve you much better.
