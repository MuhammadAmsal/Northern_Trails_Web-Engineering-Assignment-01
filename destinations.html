Cultural gateway

Lahore: begin or end with history

Many of our guests spend a day or two in Lahore before heading north. Walk the lanes of the Walled City, visit the Badshahi Mosque and Lahore Fort, and finish with dinner on one of the old food streets.

  • Half-day Walled City walking tour
  • Mughal-era monuments with a licensed historian
  • Optional cooking class with a local family
