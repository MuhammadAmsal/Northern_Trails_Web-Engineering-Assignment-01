Frequently Asked Questions

Click a question to see the answer. Still unsure? Just ask us.

When is the best time to travel?

Most routes are comfortable from May to September. Hunza is lovely from April to October, while high places such as Fairy Meadows and Deosai are best in the summer months. See the destinations page for each region.

Is it safe for solo travellers and families?

Yes. Our guides are experienced, our drivers are trained for mountain roads and every group has a 24-hour support contact. We check road and weather conditions daily and adjust plans when needed.

Do I need a visa or special permits?

Visa rules depend on your nationality, so please check with the relevant authority before booking. We arrange any local permits that your itinerary requires and tell you what documents to carry.

How fit do I need to be?

Explorer and Adventurer trips involve easy walks and long drives. The Signature package includes a multi-day hike, so a reasonable level of fitness is recommended. Tell us about any health concerns.

What happens if the weather changes the plan?

Mountain weather can close roads or delay flights. Your guide will rearrange activities, add a rest day or suggest an alternative so that you still enjoy the trip. Safety always comes first.

How do I pay and what is the cancellation policy?

We ask for a deposit to hold your dates, with the balance due before departure. Dates can be changed free of charge up to 21 days before you travel.

Will I have mobile signal and electricity?

Signal is good in towns such as Gilgit and Skardu but patchy in remote valleys. Most guesthouses have electricity, though power cuts happen, so carry a power bank and download offline maps.

What should I wear in villages and mosques?

Dress modestly: loose clothing that covers shoulders and knees is appreciated by everyone. Women may like to carry a light scarf for visiting religious sites.
