Seven Days in Hunza & Skardu

The route behind our Adventurer package, one day at a time.

  1. Day
    1

    Islamabad to Chilas

    Meet your guide at the airport and drive north along the Karakoram Highway. Stop for lunch by the Indus River and overnight in Chilas.

  2. Day
    2

    Chilas to Karimabad, Hunza

    Pause at the viewpoint for Nanga Parbat, cross into Gilgit and continue up the Hunza valley. Evening tea overlooking Baltit Fort.

  3. Day
    3

    Forts and orchards

    Visit Baltit and Altit Forts, wander the old village lanes and share a home-cooked lunch with a local family. Sunset at Eagle's Nest.

  4. Day
    4

    Attabad Lake and Passu

    Boat across Attabad Lake, walk the Hussaini suspension bridge and admire the Passu Cones. Optional drive towards Khunjerab Pass when open.

  5. Day
    5

    Hunza to Skardu

    A long scenic drive through narrow gorges where three great mountain ranges meet. Arrive in Skardu for a lakeside dinner.

  6. Day
    6

    Lakes and cold desert

    Explore the Kachura lakes, walk the dunes of Katpana and visit the old Kharpocho Fort for evening light over the Indus.

  7. Day
    7

    Return to Islamabad

    Transfer to Skardu airport for the return flight, weather permitting, or continue by road with a night stop on the way.

Please note

Mountain roads and flights depend on weather. Your guide may adjust the order of activities to keep the trip safe and enjoyable.

Packing list

What to bring

  • Layers: light fleece, windproof jacket, warm hat
  • Broken-in walking shoes with good grip
  • Sunscreen, sunglasses and a reusable water bottle
  • Power bank and a copy of your passport or ID
  • Modest clothing for village and mosque visits
